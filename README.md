# 📊 Finance Analytics Hub | Power BI

An interactive **Finance Analytics Hub** built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, costs, countries, products, and customer segments. The project transforms financial data into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards, KPI cards, charts, and slicers, helping users make data-driven business decisions.

## 📑 Project Reports

### 1. Executive Finance Overview
Provides a consolidated overview of financial performance through KPIs such as Total Sales, Total Profit, COGS, Total Units, and Profit Margin. Visualizes monthly sales trends, segment contributions, country-wise sales, and product profitability.

### 2. Sales Performance Analysis
Analyzes sales across products, countries, customer segments, and years. Includes sales heatmaps, product rankings, sales breakdowns, and contribution analysis to identify top-performing products and markets.

### 3. Profitability & Cost Analysis
Evaluates profitability by country, product, and segment. Compares monthly Sales vs. COGS and highlights profit contributions to support cost control and margin improvement.

### 4. Country & Segment Analysis
Explores country-wise sales and profitability, customer segment contributions, market distribution, and country-level comparisons to identify strong markets and business opportunities.

### 5. Finance Analytics Hub
A centralized navigation page connecting all four reports, providing an organized and user-friendly reporting experience.
